# Piezoelectric Shoes
I wanted to try making a pair of shoes that generate electricity every time I take a step. I learned about piezoelectric discs, which can turn vibrations into small amounts of electrical energy, and figured that walking creates a lot of vibrations.


The idea is to collect that energy and store it in a power bank, so it can be used later to charge a phone or another device in an emergency.

Also, I want to see what position the discs should be placed on the insole to generate the largest amount of electricty. I decided on making four versions, one with them spread out, with the other 3 concentrated in the following places, respectively: the front, middle, and heel.
